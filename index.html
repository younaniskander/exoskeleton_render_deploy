🌟 Benefits & Educational Core Targets

⚡

Active Synergy

Robotic systems usually respond after movement occurs. By utilizing EMG sensors, the robotic exoskeleton reads muscle intent, moving in perfect alignment before the leg pushes.

🔋

Reduced Energy Cost

Predicting the correct target joint angle allows actuators to apply perfect torque, drastically reducing metabolic energy expense of impaired patients during walk cycles.

🏥

Target: Right Knee Joint Angle (knee_angle_r)

The primary regression target is the Right Knee Joint Angle (in degrees). The model forecasts knee flexion/extension states ($0^\circ$ straight, up to $60^\circ$ flexed) to provide assistive power.

🔧 Connecting Real Hardware EMG Sensors

1

Attach MyoWare / BioAMP Electrodes

Place standard surface EMG electrodes on target muscle groups like your Quadriceps (Vastus Medialis/Lateralis) and Hamstrings (Biceps Femoris).

2

Wire up to Arduino / ESP32

Connect raw analog sensor outputs to ESP32 / Arduino ADC pins. Sample raw signals at 1000 Hz, bundling the data for all 11 channels.

3

HTTP Serial Stream to API

Write an ESP32 client script to POST JSON payloads containing a sliding window of 200 raw samples at the `/predict` endpoint of this live API!
